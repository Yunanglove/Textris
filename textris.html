Spell to survive

Letter tiles fall one at a time. Line them up across or down to spell words of 3+ letters and they clear.

Spawn cycle

  1. Consonant
  2. Vowel
  3. ★ Word-maker

Every 3rd tile is a vowel, and every 3–4 tiles a gold word-maker arrives: drop it in the right column and it completes a word. Q, Z, J, X and V never fall.

Every 10th tile is a blank that can be any letter. Every 20th tile is a bomb that clears a 3×3 square.
